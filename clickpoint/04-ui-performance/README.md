# UI/Performance — Angular Migration Program

**ClickPoint Software · LeadExec · Nov 2025 – Mar 2026**

---

## One-Sentence Summary

Led a discovery-driven performance program that migrated LeadExec's highest-traffic pages from legacy jQuery to Angular, eliminating persistent slowness and restoring customer confidence.

---

## My Role

I was the Product Manager for this initiative. Led evidence gathering from Clarity analytics, CS observations, and demo data. Identified and prioritised 13 hotspots. Structured engineering estimates into a phased program.

---

## Key Results

- **Lead Grid load time:** Reduced from 20–30s at peak to under 3s consistently
- **Distribution page:** Rendering stabilised — filter freezes eliminated
- **Client Details:** Page load improved across all 8 tabs; no more progressive slowdown
- **Global UI:** Toolbar lag, page transition glitches, and broken spinner resolved
- **Support tickets:** Performance-related CS escalations dropped by ~60%
- **Demo experience:** No more apologies for slow loads during prospect demos — sales team reported noticeably smoother walkthroughs
- **Customer perception:** Post-update feedback from key accounts confirmed the platform "feels faster and more responsive"
