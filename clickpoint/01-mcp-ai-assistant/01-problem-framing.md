# MCP AI Assistant — Problem Framing

## The Problem

Configuring delivery workflows in LeadExec was the highest-friction step in enterprise client onboarding. The process required creating multiple connected entities in a strict sequence: Client, Lead Type, Delivery Method, and Delivery Account.

Each step involved credentials, field mappings, time-zone logic, and validation rules. Users had to complete these steps correctly and in order, with no shortcuts or recoveries.

## The Data

- **73%** of new users reported confusion or drop-off during Delivery setup
- **40%+** of first delivery attempts failed due to missing credentials, incorrect mapping, or out-of-sequence configuration
- Only **~35%** of trial accounts reached full delivery readiness against a **75%** target
- Setup cycles extended days beyond initial signup
- SMB customers frequently lacked the technical depth to complete setup unaided

## Who It Affected

**Direct users:** New customers during trials and onboarding. These users had no prior experience with LeadExec and faced significant friction at the moment when they should have been experiencing value.

**Support teams:** Customer Success and Integrations teams were pulled into manual setup repeatedly. Onboarding became a support-intensive process that constrained growth and delayed value realisation.

**The business:** Trial activation was a critical funnel metric. When 65% of accounts weren't reaching full delivery readiness, that gap represented lost revenue and slower expansion velocity.

## Why It Mattered

LeadExec's core value proposition requires users to configure delivery — the product doesn't work without it. When setup became a blocker, the entire onboarding experience deteriorated.

The bottleneck had three effects:
1. **Activation friction** — users couldn't experience the product's core value quickly
2. **Support scaling** — manual intervention made onboarding expensive to deliver
3. **Competitive disadvantage** — simple, guided onboarding was becoming a table-stakes expectation in the market
