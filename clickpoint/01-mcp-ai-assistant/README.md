# MCP AI Assistant — Deterministic AI Agent for Enterprise Onboarding

**ClickPoint Software · LeadExec · May–Nov 2025**

---

## One-Sentence Summary

Designed and shipped an AI agent that walks enterprise clients through product setup in a single conversation, reducing onboarding time from days to under 15 minutes.

---

## My Role

I was the Product Manager for this initiative. Worked closely with the designer, lead engineer, and CS team throughout. Defined MVP boundaries, future phase direction, success criteria, and guardrails.

---

## Key Results

- **Setup time:** Reduced from days of manual setup + CS intervention to under 15 minutes via guided AI flow
- **CS intervention:** Measurable reduction in CS intervention for onboarded accounts — early production data shows users completing setup flows with less support than before
- **Safety:** Zero unsafe tool calls or unexpected AI behaviour in production
- **Real usage:** Adopted by multiple companies and users post-launch (Oct 27, 2025)
- **Foundation:** Stable MCP-to-LeadExec integration, deterministic prompt framework, and Adaptive Cards system established for the next cycle
- **Next cycle:** Scoped Client Setup Flow expansion, connection testing, telemetry dashboards, multi-session context, and Ping/Post support
