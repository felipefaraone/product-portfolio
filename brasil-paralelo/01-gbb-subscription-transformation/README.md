# GBB Subscription Model Transformation — Full-Stack Monetisation Program
**Brasil Paralelo · 2022 · 5 months across 3 Shape Up cycles · 4 concurrent squads**

---

## Problem

Brasil Paralelo sold seven content subscriptions (Patriota, BP Select, Sociedade do Livro, Clube da Música, Escola da Família, Núcleo de Formação, Acesso Total), packaged and priced as 15+ purchasable plans. Seven products, fifteen ways to buy them. All differentiated by content category, so there was no upgrade path and no clear value proposition, just a confusing menu that made acquisition harder and operations complex. Annual churn was 58%.

---

## Key Results

- **~335,634 subscribers migrated** with zero billing disruptions and zero critical outages
- **Annual revenue doubled** in the 12 months following the migration
- **Annual churn fell from 58% to 28%** (roughly 7% to 3% monthly), still trending down on departure
- 7 subscriptions and 15+ plan SKUs retired, replaced by one subscription with three tiers
- Plan distribution: Básico 251,392 · Intermediário 20,205 · Acesso Total 4K 64,037
- Projected lifetime PBT: **R$27,387,500 (~A$7.7M at 2022 rates)** on a program cost of R$70,000 (~A$20K at 2022 rates)
