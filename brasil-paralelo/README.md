# Brasil Paralelo — Product Cases

**B2C Subscription Streaming Platform · 250k to 500k+ Subscribers · 2022**

Brasil Paralelo is a Brazilian subscription streaming platform specialising in documentary and educational content, operating across web, iOS/Android, and smart TVs (Samsung, LG, Android TV). At its peak, BP had 500k+ paying subscribers and 800k+ total registered users.

I was the sole PM across a 60+ engineer organisation. No product team — I held all product discovery, prioritisation, and delivery ownership. I introduced Shape Up as the development methodology, taking the team from ad-hoc roadmaps to structured 6-week cycles with pitches, fixed appetite, and explicit rabbit holes and no-goes. Deployment frequency increased 6x.

These seven cases span the full arc of BP's 2022 commercial transformation: rebuilding the technical platform to support a new subscription model, migrating 335k subscribers to that model, and improving every part of the funnel that touched those subscribers.
